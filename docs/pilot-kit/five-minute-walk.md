**Type:** how-to · **Audience:** presenter to a platform executive · **Written:** 2026-10-14, Wave 2 / exec-layer · **Contract:** `demo-script-10min.md` (compressed); plan § 12 slice labels.

## Before you start

Open the live site (or `npm run dev` at `http://localhost:3000`). Say in the first beat: **everything on screen is synthetic** — invented merchant, menu, and statement; real rules and cited law only.

| Time | Say · do | Honest line |
| --- | --- | --- |

| **0:45–1:45** | Click **Run the bundled pair**. Read the verdict and tally **off the screen** (figures are derived; held equal to an engine run by `evals/packs/test-guide.test.ts`). | The bundled pair is **synthetic**, not any merchant’s live menu. |
