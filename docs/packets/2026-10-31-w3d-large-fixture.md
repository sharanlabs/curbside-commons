# Dispatch packet — W3-d: the large synthetic restaurant (~300 items, modifier groups, a seven-day drift series) and the alert-volume figures (Wave 3, lane 2) — 2026-10-31

**Plain English:** every number the checker has ever produced came from one twelve-item restaurant. Before anyone is shown a pilot, we need to know how noisy the checker gets on a real-sized menu: how many findings per hundred items a day of ordinary drift produces, and what share of them are mere warnings. This lane builds a bigger synthetic restaurant with its own generator and seed, runs the existing engine over seven days of seeded drift, and records those figures as derived values with a test that recomputes them. Nothing about the existing fixtures, goldens or rules changes. The drift rates are declared assumptions, labelled as such — this measures the instrument, it does not claim to know the market.

**Dispatch condition:** now, from `main` at or after the SDK bump commit. Model: Sonnet (pattern lane: it follows scripts-ts/generate-scenarios.mts and evals/packs/scenarios.test.ts; any judgment call it cannot settle from the plan is a STOP, not a guess). Worktree `.worktrees/w3d-large-fixture` on `wave3/w3d-large-fixture`, created by the orchestrator with the `node_modules` symlink in place.
